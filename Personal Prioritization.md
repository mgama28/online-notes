## The Hackathon Website Scope (Final)

### Must Have (Ship This):

```
[HEADER]
- Club name/logo
- Navigation: About | Events | Join

[HERO]
- Tagline: "DePaul's Premier Hackathon Community"
- One sentence: What you do
- CTA button: "Join Our Discord"

[ABOUT]
- 2-3 sentences about the club
- When/where you meet
- Who should join

[EVENTS]
- "Winter 2026 Events Coming Soon"
- Or list first event if planned

[JOIN/CONTACT]
- Discord link or email
- Social media links

[FOOTER]
- Copyright, links
```

**That's it. Ship this weekend.**

### Explicitly NOT Building:

- ❌ Event registration system
- ❌ User accounts/login
- ❌ Blog or news section
- ❌ Photo galleries
- ❌ Member profiles
- ❌ Complex animations
- ❌ Multiple pages

**One page. Simple. Clean. Done.**

## The Learning Platform Decision

**Put it in backlog with this framing**:

**"I'm saving this for after I secure my summer internship."**

**Why this is smart**:

- If you get PM internship: You'll learn more PM skills there than building solo
- If you get SWE internship: You'll have summer/fall to build it properly
- If recruiting takes longer: Better to focus on that than side projects

**When to revisit**:

- After you have an offer secured (March/April)
- Or Summer 2026 if you want a big project
- Or Fall 2026 with more time and experience

**It's not dead. It's strategically delayed.**
