## 🎯 Phase Goal

Understand who will use my website, what they need, and what problem it should solve. Gather real data through user research and competitive analysis.

**Key Question**: Who is this website for, and what will make it valuable to them?

---

## ✅ Phase Checklist

### Step 1: Define Your Users

- [ ]  Brainstorm all possible user types
- [ ]  Select 2-3 primary user personas
- [ ]  For each user, answer:
    - [ ]  What are they trying to accomplish?
    - [ ]  What questions do they need answered?
    - [ ]  What would make them take action?
    - [ ]  How much time will they spend?

### Step 2: User Research Planning

- [ ]  Create interview script (5-7 questions)
- [ ]  Identify 5-7 people to interview:
    - [ ]  2-3 recruiters or hiring managers
    - [ ]  2-3 peers with strong portfolios
    - [ ]  1-2 mentors or professors
- [ ]  Schedule interviews

### Step 3: Conduct Interviews

- [ ]  Interview 1: _____ (name/role) - Date: _____
- [ ]  Interview 2: _____ (name/role) - Date: _____
- [ ]  Interview 3: _____ (name/role) - Date: _____

- [ ]  Interview 5: _____ (name/role) - Date: _____

### Step 4: Competitive Analysis

- [ ]  Find 5 strong personal websites (PM or SWE candidates)
- [ ]  Document for each:
    - [ ]  What's included?
    - [ ]  How is it organized?
    - [ ]  What stands out (good/bad)?
    - [ ]  What could you borrow or avoid?

### Step 5: Synthesis

- [ ]  Review all interview notes
- [ ]  Identify common themes
- [ ]  List key insights (top 5-7)
- [ ]  Write problem statement

## 🎤 Interview Script Template

### Opening

"Hi [Name], thanks for taking time to chat. I'm building my personal website and want to make sure it actually helps [recruiters/peers/etc.] like you. I have about 5-7 questions - should take 15 minutes. Everything you say will help me build something useful. Sound good?"

### Questions

**1. Context Setting**  
"When you're evaluating a candidate, do you typically look at their personal website? Why or why not?"

**2. Behavior Understanding**  
"Walk me through what you do when you land on a candidate's website. What are you looking for?"

**3. Pain Points**  
"What's frustrating or unhelpful on most personal websites you see?"

**4. Success Criteria**  
"What would make you reach out to a candidate vs. pass? What convinces you they're worth interviewing?"

**5. Specific Feedback**  
"If you were designing my website, what would you include? What would you skip?"

**6. Mobile/Context**  
"How do you typically view these sites - mobile, desktop, during what part of your process?"

**7. Closing**  
"Anything else I should know about what makes a personal website effective?"

### Thank You

"This is super helpful. Can I share what I build with you when it's ready? I'd love your feedback."
