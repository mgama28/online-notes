> [!NOTE]
> **Idea:** Create a bento box layout for the entire screen. When the user hovers over a section, that section will lift up. Users can click on it, which will smoothly transition to another page, expanding the section to reveal more information. For example, in the "Projects" section, highlight the projects I want to showcase first. When users click to expand, they will be able to see all the additional projects.
