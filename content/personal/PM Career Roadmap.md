## 🎯 Career Paths to PM

### Option A (Most Common) ⭐ RECOMMENDED

1. Start as SWE for 1-2 years
2. Transition internally to PM (easier than external)
3. Target companies known for SWE→PM: Google, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon

### Option B (Competitive but Direct)

- Apply to APM (Associate PM) programs for new grads
- Google APM, Meta RPM, Uber APM, Salesforce APM, etc.
- Extremely competitive but worth applying

### Option C (Startup Route)

- Join early-stage startup as engineer
- Explicitly discuss desire to move toward product
- Smaller companies = more role flexibility

## ⚠️ Important Clarifications

### PM vs People Manager

- **Product Manager** ≠ People Manager
- PMs lead through influence, not authority
- You don't manage engineers directly
- People management (leading PM teams) comes 5-8+ years later
- If you want traditional management quickly, engineering management might be faster

### PM vs Technical PM (TPM)

**Product Manager**:

- Defines WHAT to build and WHY
- Focuses on user needs, market strategy, business metrics
- Works with design, marketing, sales
- Your CS background helps but role is less technical day-to-day

**Technical Product Manager**:

- Common at infrastructure/platform companies
- Focuses on technical architecture, API design, developer tools
- Works almost exclusively with engineers
- Requires deeper technical knowledge, often expects coding ability

**Recommendation**: Start with general PM (broader, more doors open)
