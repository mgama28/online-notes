### What is a Kernel?
- The middle man between software programs and the OS (the bridge)
- The heart of operating systems
#### Different Types of Kernels
- Monolithic - all services run in the kernel space
- Microkernel - keep most services out of kernel space for increased security
- Hybrid Kernel - combine elements of both

### What does the kernel do?

### Functions and Characteristics:
- Hardware Management
- Memory Management
- CPU Scheduling
- System Calls
